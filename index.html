The Supernova Foundation

I'm Gautam v.s. The Supernova Foundation is my effort to build a convergence OS — Supernova OS runs the same on mobile, tablets, and desktop, and both the desktop and mobile OS are based on Arch Linux. I build, maintain, and open-source it, and I keep it free.

I'm also working on TV OS, in collaboration with Pulsar OS (Inled group).
